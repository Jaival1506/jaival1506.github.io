Oil Market Intelligence System

A dashboard that pulls Brent crude prices, OPEC production data and oil-market news into one place. It tracks returns and volatility from 2015 to 2026, labels market regimes with Markov chains, runs Monte Carlo price scenarios and produces a 15-day probabilistic forecast.

Python, Pandas, Statsmodels, Markov chains, Monte Carlo, Streamlit

Python, yfinance, Technical indicators, Backtesting
