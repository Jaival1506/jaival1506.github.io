Oil Market Intelligence SystemCompleted, April 2026

A dashboard that pulls Brent crude prices, OPEC production data and oil-market news into one place. It tracks returns and volatility from 2015 to 2026, labels market regimes with Markov chains, runs Monte Carlo price scenarios and produces a 15-day probabilistic forecast.

Python, Pandas, Statsmodels, Markov chains, Monte Carlo, Streamlit

AI-driven modelling of crude oilResearch in progress

Tests whether adding physical supply variables improves AI and machine learning forecasts of returns and realised volatility, and whether the improvement changes between market regimes.

Machine learning, Volatility, Supply dynamics, Regime detection

An intraday scanner for NIFTY 50 stocks. It downloads market data, builds indicators such as EMA, RSI, MACD, ATR and VWAP, flags trade setups and tests them against history. I am still validating entries, targets and stop-loss logic before anything goes near a live workflow.

Python, yfinance, Technical indicators, Backtesting
